About PestGuard

Empowering agriculture through Intelligent Detection

Our Mission

PestGuard is an AI-powered pest detection and management system designed to assist farmers and agricultural experts in identifying harmful pests with high accuracy using deep learning models.

✔ Real-time identification
✔ Dual solutions (Chem/Org)
✔ Store locator
✔ Multilingual support

Our Vision

We aim to empower farmers with accessible AI technology, reduce crop losses, and promote sustainable agriculture practices. PestGuard bridges the gap between advanced machine learning models and real-world agricultural needs.
